## Generated background

`public/images/bengali-textile.webp` was generated with the built-in image-generation tool, then optimized as WebP. This is decorative artwork inspired by Bengali textiles, not a historical textile reproduction.

Final prompt:

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: landscape raster background for a Bengali Students Society website hero. Primary request: Original abstract Bengali nakshi kantha and jamdani textile-inspired artwork, with fine muted antique-gold stitched geometric floral motifs and flowing organic layers. Style/medium: Elegant contemporary editorial textile artwork, subtle handmade fabric grain and delicate embroidery. Composition/framing: Wide landscape 16:9 composition. Dark, quiet, spacious center-left reserved for cream website text; ornamental patterned edges, especially right and lower edges. Flowing organic textile layers integrated into the background. Color palette: Deep forest green and emerald green, restrained muted antique-gold stitch details. Low contrast throughout. Lighting/mood: Rich, calm, sophisticated, warmly handmade. Constraints: Background artwork only. No text, letters, logos, people, interface elements, mockup framing or watermark.

## Typography

Montserrat variable (Latin, weights 100–900), self-hosted through Next.js local fonts. Source: Fontsource 5.3.0. Font license is included at public/fonts/OFL.txt. Body text uses regular weight, navigation medium, and actions and labels bold; editorial serif headings are preserved.
